WATTx Wallet · Android · app.wattxchange.wattx_wallet

Swaps on the phone, Cake Wallet style

The plan to turn the v0.1.4 swap screen into a full in-wallet exchange. One quote sheet ranks every rail: the WATTxchange desk, THORChain, and trustless WATTxchange atomic swaps (KDF/HTLC). The 1% WATTxchange fee sits on top of each provider's own fees.

Starting point

What the app can do today

Swap screen

11 assets in the picker (WTX, WATT, BTC, ETH, LTC, DOGE, BCH, AVAX, ATOM, USDC, USDT). It gets a quote from the router and creates an order.

WTX out

The wallet signs and sends the WTX itself when you confirm. This is the only one-tap path today.

Other coins in

You get a deposit address and send from another wallet. The app holds no BTC, ETH or Polygon keys.

WATT → WTX

Hands off to the web app, because the wallet can't sign on Polygon yet.

Cake-style swap flow v0.2

~2 weeks · late Oct

Match Cake Wallet's exchange flow using the rails that already work.

  • Quote comparison sheet. List every rail by amount received, with its fee lines (network, provider, WATTxchange 1%), ETA and a custody label.
  • Fixed or floating rate where the rail offers it. Show a countdown to quote expiry and re-quote automatically when it lapses.
  • Deposit screen for coins the wallet doesn't hold. QR code and copy buttons, the THORChain memo shown large, and a refund address you must fill in.
  • Swaps tab. History with status chips (waiting, confirming, exchanging, sent, refunded) built on the existing status(id) and recentIds() calls. Local notifications on each change, kept alive by FullNodeService.
  • Limits up front. Show the desk's 100,000 WTX cap and remaining inventory before the user types an amount.
Done whenWTX→WATT works in the app end to end, and BTC→ETH works through the deposit flow.
Depends onPhase 0. Decision 1 adds extra rails here.

Multi-asset keys v0.3

~3 weeks · Nov

Cake swaps in one tap because it holds the keys for both sides. This phase gives the wallet those keys, from the seed it already has.

  • Derive more accounts from the existing BIP39 seed. BTC (BIP84), LTC, DOGE and BCH, plus one EVM account for WATT on Polygon and ETH. The app already has bip39/bip32.
  • Balances and sends through Electrum servers and public RPCs, with the same fallback pattern the desk uses for Polygon.
  • One-tap THORChain. The wallet builds the inbound transaction itself (OP_RETURN memo on UTXO chains, router deposit call on EVM), so no memo copy-paste.
  • WATT→WTX in the app. Retires the web hand-off (_webOnly).
  • Backup gate. Require a confirmed seed backup before the new accounts switch on, the lesson from the biometrics fix.
Done whenBTC→ETH and WATT→WTX complete without leaving the app.

3

WATTxchange atomic swaps on the phone v0.4

~4 weeks · Nov–Dec

Bring the trustless WATTxchange DEX into the wallet, so WTX trades against other coins without a custodian.

  • Embed the Komodo DeFi Framework (kdf). Run it on a local RPC port, the same way the app runs its full node. Decision 2 covers the SDK option.
  • Connect to the WATTxchange maker (wtx-hth-maker) and kdf-relay. Start with the order book for WTX against HTH, ALT and FLOP, then add the verified BTC, LTC and DOGE.
  • Survive app kill. Run kdf inside the foreground service. On restart it resumes in-flight swaps and refunds automatically after the locktime.
  • Same quote sheet. Atomic swaps appear next to the desk and THORChain with a “trustless” label.
  • ALT legs through the deployed Altcoinchain AtomicSwap and HTLCEscrow contracts.
Done whenA mainnet WTX⇄LTC swap completes from a phone, and a deliberately failed swap refunds by itself.
Depends onPhase 0 coin-param check and Phase 2 keys for the non-WTX leg.

WTX to everything THORChain reaches

after pool funding · Nov 2026

One quote and one order from WTX to BTC, ETH or any THORChain asset, routed through WATT and a stablecoin.

  • Route: WTX → WATT (desk) → USDC/USDT on Polygon (your pool) → THORChain.
  • Bridge the Polygon gap. As far as I know, Polygon is not a THORChain chain, so the stablecoin must move first (for example USDC over CCTP to Base or Avalanche). Verify before building. This is decision 3.
  • Multi-leg orders in the router. One order id, status per leg, and a defined refund for each leg if a later one fails.
  • THORName “nucash”. Once you register it, switch the affiliate from the full thor1… address to the short name. That frees memo bytes and lets the router drop its memo-too-long retry.
Done whenWTX→BTC runs as one order, with the combined fees shown before confirming.

Pricing

How the fee stacks

WATTxchange 1%platformFeeBps 100
Provider feeTHORChain liquidity fee · desk spread · KDF taker fee
Network feesinbound + outbound tx

WATTxchange takes 1% on every rail, on top of whatever the provider charges. The quote sheet shows each layer separately and ranks rails by what the user actually receives.

How the 1% is collected depends on the rail. THORChain pays it through the affiliate field in the memo. The desk takes it through rate.feeBps 100. Trustless atomic swaps have no middleman, so the fee needs its own collection path; that is part of decision 2.

Your call

Decisions to make

1 · Turn on ChangeNOW and SideShift?

These are Cake's own providers, and the adapters are already written. They add many non-WTX pairs. They are custodial and can hold a swap pending KYC.

SuggestEnable them in phase 1 for non-WTX pairs, labelled custodial.

2 · How to embed kdf

Bundle the kdf binary behind a local RPC, as the full node already is, or adopt Komodo's Flutter SDK. Atomic swaps also need a way to collect the 1%: a maker spread, or a dex-fee address.

SuggestBundle the binary. It matches the existing node pattern, and the maker spread covers the fee.

3 · Moving stables from Polygon to THORChain

The WTX route lands in Polygon stablecoins, which THORChain can't take directly. Options: bridge USDC over CCTP to Base or Avalanche, or seed the pool on a chain THORChain supports.

SuggestDecide before funding the pool next month. Putting the pool on a THORChain chain removes a leg.

4 · WTX wallet or multi-coin wallet

Phase 2 is the big change in scope: the app starts holding BTC, LTC, DOGE and EVM funds, and supporting them.

SuggestAdd the accounts, but keep WTX as the home screen and show the others only when they hold a balance.

Watch list

Risks

HighWrong KDF coin bytes

The engine then derives the wrong addresses and the swap fails with no error. Phase 0 checks this before any mainnet atomic swap.

HighMobile OS kills a swap mid-flight

An HTLC left unattended needs its refund claimed after the locktime. Run kdf in the foreground service and resume swaps at launch.

MedDesk inventory is custodial

Up to 100,001 WTX is committed at once. Alerts, caps and the ledger review flow already exist; keep them in front of users.

MedPartial failure on multi-leg routes

If the THORChain leg fails after the desk leg fills, the user holds WATT or stables. Each leg needs a stated refund outcome before phase 4 ships.

MedTHORChain memo length

A long affiliate address can push a memo over the limit. The router retries without the affiliate today; the THORName fixes it for good.

MedHard-coded asset list drifts

The 11 coins in SwapService.coins can list pairs with no route. Phase 0 makes the router the source of truth.
