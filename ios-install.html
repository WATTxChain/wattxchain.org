Install WATTx Wallet on your iPhone

The iPhone version isn't on the App Store yet, so you install it from a computer with a free tool called Sideloadly. It takes about 15 minutes the first time.

Download the iPhone file (.ipa) Release notes & checksum

What you need

  • Your iPhone (iOS 13 or newer) and its charging cable
  • A Mac or a Windows PC
  • Your Apple ID email and password (the normal free one is fine)
  • The wallet file WATTxWallet-v0.1.4-ios-unsigned.ipa, downloaded on the computer with the button above. Save it somewhere easy to find, like the Desktop.

Steps

  1. On the computer

    Install Sideloadly

    Go to sideloadly.io, download the version for your computer and install it.

    On Windows only: also install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website (apple.com), not from the Microsoft Store. Sideloadly needs them to see the iPhone.

  2. On the iPhone

    Plug in and trust the computer

    Connect the iPhone with the cable and unlock it. When it asks “Trust This Computer?”, tap Trust and enter your passcode.

  3. On the computer

    Install the wallet with Sideloadly

    1. Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone should appear in the iDevice box.
    2. Type your Apple ID email in the Apple account box.
    3. Drag the .ipa file onto the Sideloadly window (or click the IPA icon and pick it).
    4. Click Start. Enter your Apple ID password when asked, and the 6-digit code if Apple sends one to your devices.
    5. Wait until the bottom of the window says Done.

    Your password goes only to Apple. Sideloadly uses it to sign the app so it runs on your phone.

  4. On the iPhone

    Trust the app

    Settings › General › VPN & Device Management

    Under Developer App, tap your Apple ID email, then tap Trust and confirm.

  5. On the iPhone

    Turn on Developer Mode (iOS 16 and newer)

    Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode

    Switch it on and tap Restart. After the phone restarts, tap Turn On and enter your passcode.

    This setting only shows up after step 3 has put the app on the phone. On iOS 15 or older, skip this step.

  6. On the iPhone

    Open WATTx Wallet

    Find WATTx Wallet on your home screen and open it. Create a new wallet, or restore one with your recovery phrase.

Every 7 days: refresh it

Apps installed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. To fix it, plug the iPhone back into the computer and do step 3 again with the same Apple ID. Your wallet and its coins stay where they are.

Write down your recovery phrase when you create the wallet and keep it somewhere safe. This is a test build, so only keep small amounts in it.

If something goes wrong

“Untrusted Developer” when opening the appDo step 4 (Trust the app).
The app opens and closes straight awayOn iOS 16 or newer, do step 5 (Developer Mode). If it has been more than 7 days, refresh it.
Sideloadly doesn't show the iPhoneUnlock the phone, unplug it and plug it back in, and tap Trust if asked. On Windows, check that iTunes from apple.com is installed.
Sideloadly says the Apple ID can't be usedMake sure two-factor sign-in is on for your Apple ID and type the code it sends you. A free Apple ID can have at most 3 sideloaded apps at once; delete an old one if you hit that limit.
